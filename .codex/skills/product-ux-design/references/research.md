# Research and usability
Read when user needs are uncertain or research/usability testing is requested. Pick the question first; choose interviews, observation, task-based tests or other methods to answer it. Obtain appropriate consent, minimize personal data and keep recruitment/contact within owner authorization. Write realistic non-leading tasks; record sampling limits and observations separately from interpretations. Do not fabricate participants, findings, statistics or accessibility validation. Define task completion, errors, recovery and unresolved assumptions. Existing brief and credible evidence may suffice for a small flow change; do not force research for every UI edit. Hand off primary tasks, information hierarchy, meaningful states and content. [Task scenarios](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/task-scenarios-usability-testing/) read 2026-10-07; re-check when used.
